AltSchool teaches tech skills. There are three schools. Each school has different courses. You can learn to code, work with data, or build products.

Engineering School

Learn to build websites and apps. You will be taught how to make things work on computers and the internet. You can choose what part you like best; frontend engineering, backend engineering, cloud engineering & cybersecurity.

Product School

Learn how to make tech products people love. You will be taught how to design, sell, and manage products. It is a very good school for creative people.

Data School

Data rules the world!
Learn to work with numbers and information. Learn how to find answers in data. Good for people who are critical thinkers.
